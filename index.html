How we get this data

  • Agency list, address, phone, services: Texas HHSC directory of licensed Home and Community Support Services Agencies (HHA.xlsx). Hospice-only agencies and licenses marked EXPIRED are left out.
  • Medicare stars: CMS Care Compare — Home Health Care Agencies (quality of patient care star rating) and the Home Health patient survey (HHCAHPS) star rating, matched by Medicare number (CCN). Agencies · Survey
  • Who serves your ZIP: Medicare-certified agencies are shown only if CMS lists your ZIP in their service area (CMS ZIP file). Texas-licensed agencies with no Medicare service-area data (mostly non-medical home care) are shown if an office is within the distance you pick (10 or 25 miles, straight-line) of your ZIP's center (Census ZCTA gazetteer). Distance-based matches may not serve your address — ask.
  • Locations: branch offices are grouped under one card, by Medicare number (CCN) or by Texas license number.
  • License status: taken from the Texas HHSC directory. "Enforcement action pending" means the license is active and HHSC has an open enforcement matter; HHSC does not publish what it is. Use "Verify on Texas HHSC" to check the current status in HHSC's TULIP provider search.
  • Care types: Fact = copied from a named source. Inferred = our mapping, e.g. Texas "Personal Assistance Services" license ⇒ personal care and companionship; "Licensed Home Health Services" ⇒ skilled nursing.
  • Prices: agencies rarely publish rates, so cards say "Call for price". The area figure is a third-party survey estimate, not any agency's price.
  • Confidence (0–100): starts at 90 for government data, +10 when Texas and Medicare records agree, −20 per conflict between them, −10 per 90 days of age past 90 days. High ≥ 80, Medium 50–79, Low < 50.

Disclaimer

CareCost is an independent, free information tool. It is not affiliated with Texas HHSC or CMS, does not endorse or rank any agency, and is not medical, legal, or financial advice. Data can be out of date or wrong. Always confirm license status, services, prices, and insurance or Medicare coverage directly with the agency and the state before choosing care. Medicare stars apply only to Medicare-certified home health, not to private-pay non-medical care.
